About the data

The dashboard downloads fresh FRED observations at scheduled checks at 9:10 a.m. and 7:17 p.m. Eastern daily, including release days. The schedule follows daylight saving time. New observations appear when FRED makes them available; monthly and quarterly figures do not change every day. The refresh timestamp above records the last successful data retrieval. Scheduled runs can be delayed.

Each table labels its reporting period, units and adjustment. Monthly average job changes are the change in employment divided by the number of months between the stated endpoints. Total changes are differences between levels, not sums of employment levels.

“Since Iran War began” uses February 28, 2026 as the reference date. The table identifies the actual baseline observation for each series. Changes over this period are descriptive and do not establish that the war caused them.

Sources include BLS, BEA, Census, the Department of Labor, EIA and FRED. Stock indexes exclude dividends. Missing observations remain unavailable, and source revisions can change historical comparisons.
